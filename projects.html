Team Aegis

Verizon Smart Campus Competition, Sep 2025 to Mar 2026

An AI-powered traffic-safety system that detects reckless driving in real time and proactively adjusts traffic infrastructure to prevent collisions before they occur. We designed a four-stage pipeline on Verizon's stack: AI dashcam detection, cloud cumulative risk scoring, V2X transmission via the Edge Transportation Exchange, and signal-controller response.

We validated feasibility through primary research and direct consultation with Verizon engineers, then delivered a multi-speaker technical pitch proposing a Boston pilot deployment.

1st Place Computer Vision V2X Risk Scoring
02

Neural Style Transfer

A deep learning application built with TensorFlow and the VGG19 architecture that blends the artistic style of one image with the content of another. I used a multi-layered approach with 5 style layers and 1 content layer, optimized over 10 epochs at 100 steps per epoch using custom loss functions and the Adam optimizer.

TensorFlow VGG19 Deep Learning
03

Spotify Popularity Prediction

A predictive analytics tool forecasting song popularity with a Random Forest Regressor, achieving an RMSE of 3771.65 and outperforming a linear regression baseline at 4924.37. The model explained roughly 70% of the variability in song popularity through feature engineering, hyperparameter tuning, and visualization.

Scikit-learn Random Forest Regression
